NYC Founder Name IS Harsh Vandana Sharma

Harsh Vandana Sharma is an entrepreneur, technology educator, and developer from Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh.
He is best known as the CEO & Founder of Sheryians Coding School and is also associated with Not Your College (NYC).
Sheryians' own website lists him as its “CEO & Founder.”
According to a public GitHub profile describing his work, Harsh is a tech enthusiast and full-time learner who has focused on using technology education to help students and aspiring developers build practical skills and pursue careers in tech.

His Journey

The story behind Sheryians began around 2017.
According to a detailed account shared by Sheryians co-founder/team member Adarsh Gupta, Harsh and Adarsh met during college and wanted to create a learning community because they felt students were often learning programming without understanding how to apply it practically. They initially started teaching Web Development, allowing students to create real websites and see the results of what they were learning.
The early initiative gradually developed into Sheryians Coding School, which was founded in 2017 and is headquartered in Bhopal. Today, Sheryians describes its mission around creating tech innovators and industry-ready professionals, with an emphasis on learning, innovation and practical development.

Connection with Not Your College

Harsh Vandana Sharma is also identified publicly as the Founder of Not Your College.
Recent student/community posts describe NYC's Beyond Sigma program as a platform focused on helping students build foundations in coding, problem-solving, development and broader technology skills. The idea behind NYC can therefore be presented as extending practical, industry-oriented learning beyond traditional college education—helping students focus on skills, projects, problem solving and career preparation rather than relying only on their college curriculum.
